designated business number is missed, our platform sends an automated text
message to the caller and may capture lead information on your behalf (the
"Service"), configured per the setup information you provide.

## 4. TCPA & consent — Client responsibilities (READ CAREFULLY)
Automated text messages are regulated by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act
(TCPA) and related rules. **You are responsible for compliance with respect to
your customers**, including:
- **(a)** Ensuring you have a lawful basis / appropriate consent to send text
  messages to callers, consistent with applicable law and carrier rules.
- **(b)** Only using the Service to contact your own customers and callers, for
  legitimate purposes related to your business.
- **(c)** Providing accurate business and messaging information for A2P/10DLC
  registration.
- **(d)** Not using the Service for prohibited content (e.g., unlawful, deceptive,
  or [prohibited-industry] messaging).

The Service supports standard opt-out keywords (e.g., **STOP**) and help keywords
(**HELP**). You agree not to message anyone who has opted out, and to honor all
opt-out requests. We will process STOP requests received through the Service.
