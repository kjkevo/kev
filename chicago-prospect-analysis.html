The move that gets you hired

Hundreds of people send these companies a blank résumé. Almost nobody shows up saying "here's a real problem I found in your marketing, and here's exactly how I'd fix it in my first 90 days." Do that — in a cover letter, a LinkedIn message to the hiring manager, or a one-page mini-plan you bring to the interview — and you stop being a candidate and start being the person who already did the job.

STEP 01
Name the gap

Use the specific struggle in each card below — proof you did the homework nobody else did.

STEP 02
Bring the fix

A mini-audit, a 30/60/90 plan, or one spec sample. Small and concrete beats big and vague.

STEP 03
Aim it at a human

Find the marketing lead or recruiter on LinkedIn and send it to them directly, not the portal.

01

In-house marketing seats

These four aren't marketing companies — so their own marketing has visible gaps you can point straight at. Easiest place to walk in with a targeted "here's your problem, here's my plan" pitch and land an internal role.

American College of Surgeons

Professional association, ~95,000 members, founded 1913, Chicago HQ. Runs the annual Clinical Congress and extensive surgeon education.
★ Live role posted Membership Marketing Specialist

Their struggle

  • Recruiting & retaining younger members — residents and early-career surgeons engage on channels the legacy base doesn't.
  • Thin marketing team. They're actively hiring a Membership Marketing Specialist ($68.5–75k) — the need is real and budgeted.
  • Email-heavy, social/video-light, as most associations are.

Walk in with

  • A member-lifecycle plan: a sample onboarding + renewal + win-back email journey for early-career surgeons.
  • One social/video concept that would reach residents where email can't.
  • A 30/60/90 framed around member acquisition and retention metrics they named in the job post.
Why nowThe open req is a paid signal that budget and need are live. Answer the job description with a mini-plan and you're the obvious hire.

Covista

America's largest healthcare educator (Chamberlain, Walden, Ross, AUC). Just rebranded from Adtalem Global Education and began trading as NYSE: CVSA in Feb 2026. Greater Chicago HQ.
★ Rebrand = hiring Brand / Content / Enrollment Mktg

Their struggle

  • Brand-new name, zero search equity. "Adtalem" still owns the history, reviews, and backlinks — a live migration problem.
  • Five famous school brands under one unknown parent — the connecting story isn't built yet.
  • Enrollment demand-gen in a reputation-sensitive category.

Walk in with

  • A short rebrand-migration audit: what Adtalem→Covista needs for SEO, redirects, and reputation.
  • A LinkedIn content calendar for the new corporate brand built on the "healthcare workforce shortage" story.
  • A brand-architecture one-pager linking the five schools to the parent.
Why nowA brand-new public company mid-rebrand is building out brand, comms, and digital teams right now — perfect timing to be early.

Plante Moran

One of the largest U.S. accounting & advisory firms (founded 1924), 700+ staff in Chicago serving PE, manufacturing, real estate, healthcare, and financial services.
Marketing Mgr / Recruiting Mktg / Content

Their struggle

  • Recruiting marketing in a brutal accounting talent shortage — employer brand is a board-level problem.
  • Relationship-led, demand-gen-light — deep industry expertise that isn't packaged into modern content.
  • Differentiation vs. the Big 4 and other regionals; brand can read conservative.

Walk in with

  • An employer-brand / recruiting-marketing plan to win the talent war — the easiest "yes" they have.
  • A vertical-content sample for one of their industries (e.g., a PE or manufacturing lead-gen piece).
  • A partner-LinkedIn thought-leadership program mock-up.
AngleLead with recruiting-marketing — the talent shortage makes it the pain they feel most, and it's a whole marketing function you could own.

Utility Business Media

Niche B2B trade publisher for the utility-safety industry — Incident Prevention magazine, the iP Utility Safety Conference & Expo, and training. Founded 2004, small team, Crystal Lake IL.
Marketing / Audience / Event Mgr

Their struggle

  • Print-rooted, under-digitized. Revenue leans on magazine ads, events, and subscriptions.
  • Event marketing for the iP Conference is likely the biggest revenue lever — and a heavy lift for a lean team.
  • Thin social & SEO for the niche audience they command.

Walk in with

  • A conference registration-growth plan for iP (email + social + paid).
  • A newsletter & first-party-data idea that makes their audience more sellable to advertisers.
  • A sample media kit / sponsor one-pager.
AngleSmall team = a versatile generalist marketer stands out fast and gets real ownership on day one.
02

Agency & platform seats

Here you're hired to do the craft they sell. You stand out by demonstrating that craft — a spec campaign, a sample media plan, a portfolio piece — pointed at something real: their client, their own product, or their own brand.

Laughlin Constable

Fiercely independent full-service ad agency (Chicago + Milwaukee, ~45 years). Recently named Agency of Record for QuikTrip; second-generation creative leadership.
★ Staffing a new AOR Creative / Account / Media / Digital

Their moment

  • Just won QuikTrip as Agency of Record — new accounts mean fresh headcount, fast.
  • Independents prize versatile generalists who can flex across creative, media, and digital.
  • Their own agency marketing / new-business content often takes a back seat to client work.

Walk in with

  • A spec creative or campaign idea — ideally something for the new QuikTrip business — showing initiative and craft.
  • A capability they may not staff deeply (paid-search, analytics, motion, dev) as your differentiator.
  • A quick take on their own new-business content to show range.
Why nowThe QuikTrip win is the best timing on the whole list — agencies staff up right after landing an AOR. Move now.

Epsilon

Data-driven marketing & ad-tech company (8,000+ employees, Conversant platform), part of Publicis. A Chicago ad-tech anchor.
Product Mktg / Demand Gen / Analytics

Their struggle

  • As a product company, they need marketers to explain their platform to buyers — a genuine in-house marketing function.
  • Ad-tech firms are often better at building than positioning their own product.
  • Demand-gen, product-marketing, and event content are ongoing needs.

Walk in with

  • A product-positioning sample — reframe one Epsilon capability for a marketer audience.
  • A demand-gen or sales-enablement outline for the Conversant / loyalty products.
  • An analytics/data work sample if you're on the technical side.
AngleA big company with a large in-house marketing org — you can genuinely do B2B/product marketing here, not just service clients.

Later

Social & influencer-marketing platform that acquired Chicago's Mavely (creator-commerce) for ~$250M. HQ outside Chicago, real Chicago footprint via Mavely.
Product / Creator & Community / Content

Their struggle

  • Post-acquisition integration — merging Later + Mavely brand, positioning, and creator communities is unfinished work.
  • As a platform, they need product marketing and creator-community content.
  • Growth mode after a $250M deal usually means expanding teams.

Walk in with

  • A creator-community content idea that uses their tools the way their users would.
  • A positioning take on uniting Later + Mavely for creators and brands.
  • Proof you live in the creator economy — your own social/content is the portfolio here.
AngleIf you know the creator economy, the Mavely integration is a timely, specific hook few applicants will mention.

Publicis Health Media (PHM)

Market-leading health & wellness media agency (~$225M revenue, ~700 staff), 35 W Wacker. Pharma/biotech media planning & buying; aggressive AI adoption.
Media Planner / Analyst / Search

What they hire for

  • Growing revenue & headcount — a steady pipeline of media and analytics roles.
  • They value people who can navigate regulated pharma media (MLR, compliance).
  • AI-forward workflows — comfort with new tools is a real edge.

Walk in with

  • A spec media plan or channel strategy for a health/wellness brand.
  • A data/analytics sample showing you can measure and optimize.
  • A line on how you'd use AI to speed planning — matches where they're heading.
AngleSpecialize your pitch toward regulated health/pharma media — that focus is exactly what sets applicants apart here.

OMD

Global media agency within Omnicom Media Group; Chicago office at 225 N Michigan Ave. Media planning & buying at scale.
Media Planner / Buyer / Analyst

What they hire for

  • Large media shop with high-volume entry & mid-level hiring.
  • Client wins drive bursty demand for planners and analysts.
  • Values fluency with planning tools and measurement.

Walk in with

  • A spec media plan for a recognizable brand — audience, channels, budget logic.
  • Evidence of tool fluency (Nielsen, Google, DSPs, Excel modeling).
  • Target individual account teams and recruiters on LinkedIn, not just the careers portal.
AngleOne of the highest-volume hirers on the list — a clean spec media plan gets you past the pile fast.

Publicis Groupe

Global advertising holding company; major Chicago presence through Leo Burnett and Starcom in the Leo Burnett Building.
Apply to the operating agencies

How to target it

  • "Publicis Groupe" is the holding company — you don't get hired there. You get hired at Leo Burnett, Starcom, PHM, Epsilon, etc.
  • Big agencies have constant churn — entry and mid roles open all the time.
  • They hire on demonstrated craft, not just résumés.

Walk in with

  • A spec campaign for one of the agency's real clients — the single best portfolio move.
  • Applications aimed at the named operating agency and its recruiters, not the corporate page.
  • A clear craft focus (creative, strategy, media, paid-ops) so they know where you slot in.
AngleTreat "Publicis" as a doorway to Leo Burnett and Starcom — apply to the agency, with spec work for its clients.

Logical Media Group

Full-service digital agency in River North (~39 staff, ~$4M revenue, founded 2006). SEO, PPC, Amazon, web design, social, email.
SEO / PPC Specialist · Account · Web

What they hire for

  • Small, growth-oriented shop — specialists get hired and grow into more, fast.
  • Performance-focused: they value hands-on Google Ads / Analytics skill.
  • A Google Partner agency — certifications carry real weight.

Walk in with

  • Your Google Ads & Analytics certifications front and center.
  • A sample audit of a real brand's paid-search or SEO — concrete, hands-on proof.
  • A small case study where you moved a metric (even a personal or volunteer project).
AngleAt a ~39-person shop, a real sample audit lands directly on the desk of someone who can hire you.
