# Missed Call Text-Back Automation - Business Pitch

## The Problem
Local service businesses (plumbers, HVAC, electricians, contractors) miss calls every day. When a potential customer gets voicemail, they often:
- Call a competitor who answers
- Text someone else
- Forget about the business entirely

**Result: Lost revenue, poor customer satisfaction**

---

## The Solution
Our automated system instantly texts customers who miss your call:

> "Sorry we missed your call! ABC Plumbing will call you back shortly. Reply here if you'd like to send details now."

**Result: 2-3x more callbacks, faster response times, better customer experience**
