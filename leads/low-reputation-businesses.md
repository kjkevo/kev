# Low-Reputation Home Service Businesses

A running log of home service and trade businesses with weak online reputations.
Each batch is 10 **new** companies. Before building a new batch, check every name
in this file (including "Checked and excluded") and don't repeat any of them.

Ratings come from search-engine snapshots of Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB,
PissedConsumer and ComplaintsBoard, taken on the batch date. The review sites
couldn't be opened directly, so check a listing yourself before contacting the business.
